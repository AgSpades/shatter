# 🚶 Shatter — Game Walkthrough (High-Level Arc)

This document outlines the player's journey from awakening to the final confrontation, integrating gameplay mechanics with narrative beats.

---

## 🌑 Prologue: The Disposal Pit

**Theme:** _Claustrophobia, Confusion, Fragility._

1.  **Awakening**: The player controls **The Shell** (Aryn), waking up in a pile of discarded, broken husks at the bottom of the world `(y=2000)`.
2.  **The Glitch**: Aryn realizes he isn't dead. His "Emotion Filter" is cracked.
3.  **Tutorial (Movement)**:
    - Learn to **Scuttle** (Move) and **Flutter** (Jump).
    - The player feels heavy and weak.
    - **Goal**: Climb out of the pit before the "Sanitization Squad" (Incinerators) activates.

---

## 🏭 Act 1: The Underhive (The Ascent)

**Theme:** _Industry, Heat, Oppression._

1.  **The Climb**: A vertical platforming section through the industrial roots of Nivara.
2.  **Hazard Introduction**:
    - **Red Zones**: Leaking steam/heat vents. Contact causes damage.
    - **Enforcers**: Patrol drones that spray "cement" to fix cracks. Player must use stealth (hiding in shadows) to avoid them.
3.  **The Discovery**: Aryn finds a dead member of "The Unmasked" and scavenges a **Grey Mask** (Stealth/Silence).
    - _Unlock_: Ability to "blend in" with shadows.

---

## 🏙️ Act 2: The City of Nivara

**Theme:** _False Beauty, Silence, Social Horror._

1.  **Arrival**: The player emerges into the city proper. It is beautiful, glowing with amber light, but eerily silent.
2.  **Social Stealth**:
    - Walk among NPCs (Worker Drones).
    - **Mechanism**: If you run or jump near them, they get "stressed" (visualized by color shifts). If they panic, Enforcers are summoned.
    - **Goal**: Reach the Technician's Guild without causing a scene.
3.  **The Choice**: Aryn encounters a "Leaker" (someone crying/leaking Blue pheromones).
    - _Option A_: Report them (Stay safe/Loyal).
    - _Option B_: Help them hide (Gain **Blue Mask** shard - Time Slow).

---

## 🕸️ Act 3: The Spire (The Truth)

**Theme:** _Bio-Horror, Revelation, Power._

1.  **Infiltration**: Entering the Royal Spire where the Pheromones are manufactured.
2.  **The Twist**: The "Pheromones" aren't medicine; they are liquid mind-control derived from the Queen's own blood.
3.  **Gameplay Escalation**:
    - Use **Red Mask** (Rage) to break barriers.
    - Use **Black Mask** (Hate) to see hidden truths/platforms.
    - **Risk**: The **Metamorphosis Meter** rises. If it hits 100%, Aryn momentarily turns into a feral beast (uncontrollable damage dealer).

---

## 👑 Finale: The Queen's Chamber

**Theme:** _Judgment, Sacrifice._

1.  **Confrontation**: Aryn reaches **Apex Sol** (The Queen).
2.  **The Final Choice** (Endings):
    - **Ending 1 (Control)**: Aryn repairs his mask, submits, and becomes the new Captain of the Guard. The Cycle continues.
    - **Ending 2 (Chaos)**: Aryn shatters the Queen's tank. The pheromones stop. The city wakes up screaming (Raw Emotion).
    - **Ending 3 (Balance)**: Aryn replaces the Queen but changes the formula to allow _some_ emotion. A hard, long peace.

---

## 🔄 Core Loop Summary

1.  **Traverse** dangerous environments (Platforming).
2.  **Observe** NPCs and environmental cues (Story).
3.  **Switch Masks** to solve puzzles or survive hazards.
4.  **Manage Sanity/Metamorphosis** to avoid losing control.
