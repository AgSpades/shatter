## 🎭 **Title: *“Shatter”***

### 🌍 **World Setting**

In the city of **Nivara**, everyone wears a **mask**.

Not for festivals.
Not for fun.

It’s the law.

Long ago, a disaster called **“The Shatter”** made people lose control of their emotions. Rage, fear, jealousy could physically hurt others.

So the rulers created **Emotion Masks**:
Each mask suppresses a feeling.

* 😐 Grey Mask → No emotion
* 😠 Red Mask → Anger sealed
* 😢 Blue Mask → Sadness sealed
* 😈 Black Mask → Hate sealed

People became “safe”…

…but empty.

---

### 🧍‍♂️ Main Character

You play as **Aryn** (rename as you like):

A young technician who repairs broken masks.

One day, Aryn finds a **cracked mask**.

When he wears it…

He can feel again. ❤️🔥😨

For the first time in his life.

---

### 🕵️ Story Twist

The cracked mask lets Aryn:

* See **hidden memories**
* Hear **people’s true thoughts**
* Detect **lies**
* Sense **suppressed emotions**

He realizes:

> The disaster never ended.
> It was hidden.

The rulers use masks to **control minds**.

---

### ⚔️ Core Conflict

There is an underground group called **“The Unmasked”**.

They believe:

> “Pain is better than fake peace.”

They want to destroy the mask system.

But if masks are destroyed suddenly…

The city may collapse.

---

### 🎮 Gameplay Connection

Each level = one emotion

| Level | Mask Type | Ability      |
| ----- | --------- | ------------ |
| 1     | Grey      | Stealth mode |
| 2     | Red       | Power boost  |
| 3     | Blue      | Time slow    |
| 4     | Black     | Shadow walk  |

You switch masks during gameplay.

But…

Every switch drains your sanity bar 🧠⚠️

Use too much → lose control.

---

### 💥 Ending Choices (Multiple Endings)

Player decides:

#### Ending 1: Control

Keep masks.
Rule peacefully.
Become next tyrant.

#### Ending 2: Freedom

Destroy system.
Chaos.
But real life.

#### Ending 3: Balance (True Ending)

Rebuild masks.
Let people choose.

Hardest path.
